Most issues in Just Spades can be resolved quickly without contacting support. This guide covers the most frequently reported problems and walks you through the most effective fixes for each one. Work through the steps in order — the first step resolves the issue in the majority of cases.

<Tip>
  Before diving into specific issues, try two universal fixes that resolve a wide range of problems: **clear the app cache** (Android: Settings → Apps → Just Spades → Storage → Clear Cache; iOS: offload and reinstall the app) and ensure you are running the **latest version** of Just Spades from the App Store or Google Play. Outdated versions are the most common source of crashes, visual glitches, and connectivity failures.
</Tip>

## Common Issues

<Accordion title="App Crashes on Launch">
  If Just Spades closes immediately or freezes on the loading screen, follow these steps:

  1. **Force-close and reopen the app.** On iOS, swipe up from the home bar and swipe the app away. On Android, tap the recents button and dismiss Just Spades. Relaunch the app normally.
  2. **Restart your device.** A full device restart clears memory pressure that can prevent the app from loading correctly.
  3. **Check for app updates.** Open the App Store or Google Play, search for Just Spades, and install any available updates. Crashes on launch are often fixed in the latest release.
  4. **Reinstall the app.** Delete Just Spades from your device and reinstall it fresh from the store. Your account data is stored server-side and will be restored when you sign back in.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Connection Issues and Mid-Game Disconnects">
  Just Spades requires a stable internet connection for online multiplayer. If you're experiencing dropped connections or are unable to join games, try the following:

  1. **Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.** If you're on Wi-Fi, temporarily switch to cellular data (or vice versa) to determine whether the issue is network-specific.
  2. **Move closer to your router.** Weak Wi-Fi signals cause packet loss that can disconnect you from live matches. A closer position or a 5 GHz band connection improves stability.
  3. **Check Just Spades server status.** Occasionally, server maintenance or outages affect multiplayer availability. Visit the Just Spades social channels or status page for real-time updates.
  4. **Disable VPN or proxy services.** VPNs can route your traffic in ways that exceed the game's connection timeout thresholds. Disable any active VPN and attempt to reconnect.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Game Freezes Mid-Round">
  If the game stops responding during a match — cards won't play, the timer is stuck, or nothing responds to taps — use these steps:

  1. **Wait 10–15 seconds.** Brief freezes can occur during heavy server load and often resolve on their own. Do not force-close the app immediately.
  2. **Check your internet connection.** Open a browser or another app to confirm your device still has network access. If connectivity dropped, reconnecting may resume the game automatically.
  3. **Force-close and reopen Just Spades.** The game saves your hand and current round state server-side. When you relaunch and sign back in, you should be returned to the active match in progress.
  4. **If the match cannot be rejoined,** the round may have been abandoned by the server after a timeout. Check your match history — abandoned games resulting from server errors are typically not counted as losses on your record.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can't Sign In to Your Account">
  If you are unable to sign in, follow these steps based on your sign-in method:

  1. **Verify your credentials.** Ensure you are entering the correct email address and password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
  2. **Reset your password.** Tap **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen and follow the reset email instructions. Check your spam or junk folder if the email does not arrive within a few minutes.
  3. **Check your social login provider.** If you use Apple ID or Google sign-in, make sure you are signed into the correct Apple or Google account on your device before tapping the social login button in Just Spades.
  4. **Clear the app cache and retry.** Cached login tokens can occasionally become corrupted. Clear the cache (Android) or reinstall the app (iOS) and attempt to sign in again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Missing Purchases or Coins Not Credited">
  If you completed a purchase but your coin balance did not update, do the following:

  1. **Wait a few minutes and refresh.** Store transactions can take up to 5 minutes to process and credit. Close the purchase screen, wait, then return to the coin balance view.
  2. **Use the Restore Purchases option.** Navigate to **Settings → Account → Restore Purchases** and tap **Restore**. This re-validates all prior transactions against the App Store or Google Play and re-credits any items that were not applied.
  3. **Confirm the payment completed.** Check your Apple ID or Google account purchase history to confirm the transaction was charged. If the charge appears but coins are still missing after restoring, contact support with your transaction receipt.
  4. **Do not purchase again.** Avoid re-buying the same item before the issue is resolved. Duplicate purchases through the same storefront are typically non-refundable.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Audio Not Working">
  If game sounds, music, or UI audio effects are not playing, work through these checks:

  1. **Check your device volume.** Ensure your device is not on silent or vibrate mode, and that the media/game volume is turned up. On iOS, check the Ring/Silent switch on the side of the device.
  2. **Check in-app audio settings.** Go to **Settings → Sound** within Just Spades and verify that Sound Effects and Music toggles are both enabled.
  3. **Check if another app is using audio.** If a music or podcast app is running in the background, it may be claiming audio focus. Close other audio apps and relaunch Just Spades.
  4. **Restart the app.** Force-close Just Spades and reopen it to reset the audio session, which resolves most audio-related issues on both iOS and Android.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  If none of the steps above resolve your issue, the Just Spades support team is ready to help. Visit the [Contact Support](/support/contact) page to submit a request — include your device model, OS version, and a description of the problem for the fastest resolution.
</Note>
