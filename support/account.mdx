Your Just Spades account is the hub for your player profile, match history, earned coins, and personalized settings. Whether you're setting up for the first time or making changes to an existing account, this guide walks you through every option available in the app.

## Creating a New Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Just Spades and Tap Sign Up">
    Launch the app and select **Create Account** on the welcome screen. You'll be offered two registration paths: signing up with an email address and password, or continuing with a linked social account (Apple ID or Google).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details">
    If registering with email, provide a valid email address and choose a secure password of at least eight characters. You'll also be asked to pick an initial **display name** — this is how other players will see you at the table. Display names must be between 3 and 20 characters and cannot contain special characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    Just Spades will send a verification link to the address you provided. Open the email and tap **Verify My Account** to activate your profile. You have 24 hours to complete verification before the link expires — if it does, you can request a new one from the sign-in screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Avatar">
    After verification, you'll be prompted to choose a starting avatar from the built-in collection. Avatars can be changed at any time from your profile settings. Additional avatar styles and animated icons can be unlocked using in-game coins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Playing">
    Once your account is active, you're taken directly to the home screen. Your stats tracking, coin balance, and match history all begin from this point forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account Settings

<Accordion title="Signing In and Social Login">
  To sign in on a new device, open Just Spades and tap **Sign In** on the welcome screen. Enter your registered email and password, or tap the Apple or Google button if you used social login during registration.

  You can link or unlink social login providers at any time by navigating to **Settings → Account → Linked Accounts**. Linking an additional provider (for example, adding Google to an email account) gives you more sign-in options without creating a duplicate profile.

  If you forget your password, tap **Forgot Password** on the sign-in screen. A reset link will be emailed to your registered address and expires after one hour.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Updating Your Display Name and Avatar">
  To change your display name, go to **Profile → Edit Profile** and tap the name field. Enter your preferred name and tap **Save**. Display name changes are limited to once every 7 days to maintain a consistent experience for other players.

  To update your avatar, tap your current avatar image on the Profile screen. The avatar gallery will open, showing all available options. Free avatars are accessible immediately; premium avatars show their coin cost and can be purchased from the same screen.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Notification Settings">
  Just Spades sends push notifications for events such as game invitations, turn reminders in async matches, and promotional offers. To manage these, go to **Settings → Notifications**.

  Available notification controls:
  - **Game Invitations** — Alerts when a friend or matched player invites you to a game.
  - **Your Turn Reminders** — Notifies you when it is your turn in an active game.
  - **Promotional Offers** — Alerts about coin bonuses, limited-time events, and special deals.
  - **System Announcements** — App updates and maintenance notices (recommended to keep enabled).

  You can also manage notification permissions at the device level through your phone's **Settings → Just Spades → Notifications** menu.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="In-App Purchases and Coins">
  Just Spades uses an in-game coin currency that can be earned through gameplay or purchased directly via the App Store or Google Play. Coins are used to unlock premium avatars, table themes, and card back designs.

  To purchase coins, tap the **coin icon** in the top navigation bar and select a coin bundle. All purchases are processed securely through your device's native payment system and are subject to the platform's refund policies.

  To review your purchase history, go to **Settings → Account → Purchase History**. If a purchase was completed but coins were not credited, see the [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) guide for steps to restore missing purchases.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Deleting Your Account">
  To permanently delete your Just Spades account, navigate to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. You will be asked to confirm your decision by entering your password and tapping **Confirm Deletion**.

  Account deletion is irreversible and removes your profile, match history, friends list, and purchased cosmetic items from our servers.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  After account deletion, Just Spades retains anonymized, non-identifiable gameplay data for up to **30 days** for operational and fraud-prevention purposes, after which all associated records are permanently purged. Any unused coin balance or purchased items are forfeited upon deletion and cannot be refunded or transferred.
</Note>
