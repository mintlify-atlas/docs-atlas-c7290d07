The Just Spades support team is here to help with anything the in-app help resources haven't resolved — from account issues and missing purchases to technical bugs and feedback. Providing the right details upfront means we can diagnose and resolve your issue in a single reply, saving you time.

<Info>
  Before reaching out, check the [Troubleshooting guide](/support/troubleshooting) — it covers the most common issues including app crashes, connectivity problems, sign-in failures, and missing purchases with step-by-step fixes you can apply right now.
</Info>

## How to Reach Support

You can contact the Just Spades support team by email at **support@justspades.com**. We monitor this inbox every business day and prioritize requests that include complete device and issue information.

<Note>
  Our typical response time is **1–2 business days**. Requests submitted over weekends or public holidays will be handled on the next available business day. For the fastest resolution, make sure your email includes all of the information listed in the steps below — incomplete requests may require a follow-up reply before we can begin investigating.
</Note>

## Submitting an Effective Support Request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Include Your Device Information">
    Start your email with your device details so our team can immediately identify any platform-specific factors. Include:
    - **Device model** (e.g., iPhone 15, Samsung Galaxy S24, iPad Air)
    - **Operating system and version** (e.g., iOS 17.4, Android 14)
    - **Just Spades app version** (found under Settings → About in the app)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the Issue Clearly">
    Write a clear, step-by-step description of what happened. Include:
    - What you were doing when the issue occurred (e.g., "I was placing a bid in an online match")
    - What you expected to happen versus what actually happened
    - How frequently the issue occurs (every time, occasionally, only once)
    - Any error messages or codes displayed on screen — copy these exactly as they appear
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach Screenshots or Screen Recordings">
    Visual evidence dramatically speeds up diagnosis. Take a screenshot of any error message, unexpected screen state, or visual glitch and attach it to your email. If the issue involves motion (a freeze, animation bug, or UI behavior), a short screen recording is even more helpful. Most devices allow screen recording from the Control Center or Quick Settings panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include Your Account Details">
    To look up your account, we'll need either your **registered email address** or your **in-game display name**. Do not share your password in your support email — we will never ask for it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Your Email">
    Send your complete request to **support@justspades.com** with a subject line that briefly describes the issue (e.g., "Missing coins after purchase – iOS" or "App crashes on launch – Android 14"). A confirmation reply will be sent automatically when your ticket is received.
  </Step>
</Steps>
