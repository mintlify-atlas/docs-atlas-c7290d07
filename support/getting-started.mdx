Just Spades brings the timeless card game of Spades to your mobile device, whether you want to sharpen your skills against AI opponents or compete in real-time online matches against players around the world. Getting up and running takes only a few minutes — here's everything you need to know to play your first hand.

<Note>
  Just Spades is available on both **iOS** (App Store) and **Android** (Google Play). The experience is fully cross-platform, so iOS and Android players can compete against each other in online multiplayer matches.
</Note>

## Setting Up Just Spades

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the App">
    Open the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android) on your device and search for **Just Spades**. Tap **Get** or **Install** to download the app — it's free to install and will appear on your home screen once the download is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or Sign In to Your Account">
    When you launch Just Spades for the first time, you'll be prompted to sign in or create a new account. You can register with your email address or use a social login option such as Apple ID or Google. Returning players can sign in immediately to restore their stats, coins, and progress across devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Game Mode">
    From the home screen, select the mode that suits you best:

    - **Solo vs AI** — Play a full four-player Spades game where the other three seats are filled by computer opponents. Great for learning the rules, experimenting with bidding strategies, or just enjoying a quick game offline.
    - **Online Multiplayer** — Join a live table with real players matched by skill level. You'll be paired with a partner and face two opponents in a standard partnership Spades match.

    Additional modes and seasonal challenges may appear on the home screen as they are unlocked or introduced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deal and Play Your First Hand">
    Once a game begins, the deck is automatically shuffled and dealt — 13 cards to each player. Review your hand, place your bid when it's your turn, then follow the lead card each trick. Spades are always the trump suit, so plan your bids and plays accordingly. The first team to reach the target score wins the match.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Game Modes at a Glance

**Solo vs AI** is the ideal starting point for players who are new to Spades or just want to practice without the pressure of a live match. The AI scales to provide a fair challenge and is available at any time, even without an internet connection.

**Online Multiplayer** pairs you with a randomly assigned partner against two other real players. Communication with your partner is handled through in-game emotes and bid signals, keeping the game both social and competitive. Your wins, losses, and overall record are tracked on your player profile.

<Tip>
  If you've never played Spades before, tap the **Tutorial** button on the home screen before jumping into your first game. The interactive tutorial walks you through bidding, trick-taking, and trump rules with on-screen guidance so you'll feel confident from the very first hand.
</Tip>
