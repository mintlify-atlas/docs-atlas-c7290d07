## The Game of Spades

Spades is a classic four-player partnership card game played with a standard 52-card deck. Each round, players bid on the number of tricks they expect to win, then work with their partner to meet that bid exactly. Spades are always trump — meaning any Spade beats cards of any other suit — which makes every hand a careful balance of strategy, communication, and a little bit of luck.

A typical game unfolds in three stages:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bidding">
    Before a single card is played, each player predicts how many of the 13 tricks they expect to win. Bids are added together within each partnership to form a team target.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Playing Tricks">
    Players take turns leading and following suit. Spades can be played once the suit has been "broken," and the highest card of the led suit — or the highest Spade — wins each trick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scoring">
    Teams score points for meeting their bid and lose points for falling short. Winning more tricks than bid earns "bags" — accumulate too many and your score takes a penalty.
  </Step>
</Steps>
