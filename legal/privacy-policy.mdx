Just Spades is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play Just Spades on any supported mobile device. Please read this policy carefully before using the app.

<Note>
  **Effective: January 1, 2025.** By downloading or using Just Spades, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.
</Note>

<Info>
  Have questions about your privacy or want to submit a data request? Visit our [Contact & Support](/support/contact) page to reach our privacy team directly.
</Info>

## Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate and improve Just Spades. This falls into the following categories:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Account Information" icon="user">
    When you register, we collect your username, email address, and (if applicable) your linked social login identifiers from Google or Apple Sign-In. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gameplay Data" icon="gamepad">
    We record game sessions, scores, win/loss records, bid histories, and in-game achievements to power leaderboards, statistics, and personalized experiences.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Device Information" icon="mobile">
    We automatically collect your device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and mobile network information to ensure compatibility and detect fraud.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Analytics & Usage Data" icon="chart-bar">
    We collect information about how you interact with the app — screens visited, features used, session duration, and crash reports — to diagnose issues and improve the game.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

We do **not** collect payment card numbers directly. Any in-app purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play and governed by their respective privacy policies.

## How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively to deliver and improve the Just Spades experience:

- **Operate the game:** Authenticate your account, save progress, maintain leaderboards, and enable multiplayer matchmaking.
- **Improve the service:** Analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, and develop new features based on how players engage with the game.
- **Send notifications:** With your permission, send push notifications about game updates, tournament invitations, and rewards. You can opt out at any time in your device settings.
- **Ensure fair play:** Detect and prevent cheating, fraudulent activity, and unauthorized access to our systems.
- **Legal compliance:** Meet our obligations under applicable laws and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

<Accordion title="How do we use analytics data specifically?">
  Analytics data (collected via Firebase) is used in aggregate to understand feature adoption and app stability. We do not use analytics to build individual advertising profiles. Analytics identifiers are pseudonymous and are not combined with your account email address for marketing purposes.
</Accordion>

## Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service providers:** We share necessary data with trusted vendors — such as Google Firebase for analytics and crash reporting, and cloud hosting providers — who are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only on our behalf.
- **Legal requirements:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal processes (e.g., a court order or government request).
- **Business transfers:** If Just Spades is acquired by or merged with another company, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
- **Protection of rights:** We may share information when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Just Spades, our players, or the public.

<Info>
  Firebase Analytics is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can learn more about how Google uses data at [google.com/policies/privacy/partners](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
</Info>

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Just Spades service.

<Accordion title="What happens when I delete my account?">
  When you request account deletion, we permanently remove your profile, gameplay history, and associated personal data within **30 days**. Certain anonymized, aggregate data (e.g., total games played statistics) may be retained indefinitely as it cannot be linked back to you. Backup copies may persist for up to **90 days** before being purged from our systems.
</Accordion>

We may retain certain data longer where required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our Terms of Service.

## Children's Privacy

Just Spades is **not directed at children under the age of 13** and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our service complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete that information. If you believe your child has submitted personal data to us, please contact us at [support@justspades.com](mailto:support@justspades.com) and we will promptly investigate and remove the data.

## Your Rights

Depending on your country or state of residence, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction:** Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Deletion:** Request that we delete your account and personal data (subject to legal retention obligations).
- **Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Opt-out of communications:** Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in any email or through your in-app notification settings.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at [support@justspades.com](mailto:support@justspades.com). We will respond to verified requests within **30 days**.

<Note>
  Residents of California (CCPA), the European Economic Area (GDPR), and other jurisdictions with specific privacy laws may have additional rights. Contact us to learn more about the rights applicable to your location.
</Note>

## Security

We take reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

- All data transmitted between the Just Spades app and our servers is encrypted using **TLS (Transport Layer Security)**.
- Account passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and are never stored in recoverable form.
- Access to production systems and personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis.
- We conduct periodic security reviews and vulnerability assessments of our infrastructure.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will:

- Post the revised policy with an updated **"Last Updated"** date at the top of this page.
- Send an in-app notification or email (for significant changes) at least **14 days** before the changes take effect.

Your continued use of Just Spades after the effective date of any revised policy constitutes your acceptance of the changes. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
